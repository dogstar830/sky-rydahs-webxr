## 10. VR games research (Oliver, Muse page "Top VR games — and how their gameplay was actually built")
Source: https://muse.ai/s/top-vr-games-how-their-gameplay-was-xuxu66x0xlxcxkxkxx8/tXJSgjPlm7hadN7cy5VPRg8PMziU_eUQawn-3w4-pfU (full text saved in refs/vr-games-research.txt)
Character-art takeaways:
- In VR, show the player's hands and skates only (no full body): build a separate first-person rig of gloves/forearms + skates per rider.
- Cel-shade is a performance strategy: in VR, no post-processing, no shadow maps; sprite glow instead of bloom; readable silhouettes.
- Draw calls limit the city: aim for 1–3 draw calls per NPC rider (merge meshes, one atlas), outlines included; use LODs.
- Body as inventory: spray cans/boost on hips, wrist radio, glove status display should be modeled on the riders.
- Avoid long held rigid props (crowbar lesson) for spray cans/batons.

## 11. Modular avatar / Roblox design research (optimization pass)
Source notes: [refs/roblox-design-research.md](refs/roblox-design-research.md)
Applied in `shared/rider_lib.py` + `shared/rider_loader.js`:
- Named attachment empties `*_Att` (Hat, Hair, Face, Neck, Collar, ShoulderL/R, BodyFront, BodyBack, Waist, GripL/R, FootL/R), parented to bones and exported; exposed as `rider.userData.attachments`.
- Modular parts kept as separate GLB meshes (`<P>_LOD0_body|hair|top|skates|accessory|glow`) with slot tri budgets: body ≤10k, hair ≤2.5k, top ≤3k, skates ≤2k, accessory ≤1k per item. They are merged into one SkinnedMesh at load.
- Atlas cells map to palette roles: primary→jacket, secondary→cloth, accent→gold+iris, trim→skate, skin→skin (`crewColors` accepts role or slot keys).
- Max 4 bone influences per vertex (limit_total + normalize).

## 15. Cyberpunk 2077 / Edgerunners research → Caali hero pass
Source notes: [refs/cyberpunk2077-design-research.md](refs/cyberpunk2077-design-research.md) (Characters section).
I used the formula *era + one signature motif + one silhouette trait + a 2–3 colour palette* and checked silhouette before colour. For Caali / Code : E that is the "corpo" era: sharp black with gold trim lines.
- **Hero garment** (in the way David's jacket carries David): the Code : E *storm jacket*. It is the `herojacket` accessory item in `code-e/src/build_rider.py` and has:
  - an angular split storm collar with gold edges
  - blade shoulder lines that break the shoulder silhouette
  - gold trim lines on the front panels
  - a swallowtail back hem with gold edging
- **Crew emblem on the back for the chase cam:** the big gold bolt, now framed by two angular gold brackets that make a crest. See `renders/back.png` and `sil_back.png`.
- **Silhouette first:** checked as a black shape in `renders/sil_34.png` and `sil_back.png`. The collar, blade shoulders and swallowtail all show in the outline.
- **Flat Trigger-style shading:** `shared/rider_loader.js` now defaults to a 3-tone ramp with nearest filtering (`opts.tones` can be 2, 3 or 4). Saturated neon stays limited to the emissive slots, and the inverted-hull outline is unchanged.

## 17. Hero pass for the seven crews (Oct 28, 2026)
The goal was to bring every crew to the same level as Caali. The shared kit is `shared/hero_face.py` (anime head, 8 expression shape keys, clumped hair). `shared/upgrade_hero.py` holds each crew's face and hair parameters, and `shared/fix_open_items.py` holds the SF/Genesis/TTT fixes.

**Canon sheets used (style and appearance reference only, no copied characters):**
- **Sheet 10 (Halo / Static Angels):**
  - navy twin tails with purple ties, purple eyes, cyan cropped jacket, black cargos with purple straps, a chain, the halo, glowing wheels.
  - Sheet 10 shows **no wings and no battery pack**, so both were removed.
- **Sheet 09 (Anubis):** jackal helmet with cyan optics, tactical armor, LED strips. The optics are now emissive `iris`-slot LED flats that carry the expression morphs.
- **Sheet 11 (crew sheet):** used for silhouette and proportion checks only.

**Real-world grounding for the fixes (general knowledge, no new web sources):**
- **Flip-up welding helmet** (ANSI Z87 / Miller-style): a flat-faced shell, a rectangular dark filter-lens window, a ratchet headgear band and big side pivot knobs. Tool Toul To now has these, with the helmet flipped up about 58°.
- **Sleeping Forest's longcoat:** a flared, A-line tailored coat skirt with front, back and side vents (frock-coat / haori hybrid). It replaces the flat panels.
- **Wrap shield sunglasses** (sport shield style): a thin single-lens band that follows the face curve. It replaces Genesis's chunky shades.
