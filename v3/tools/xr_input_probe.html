2-minute procedure
  1. Pair the Xbox pad in Quest settings if you will test it. Confirm it appears in the live readout below.
  2. Tap ENTER VR. Follow the prompts on the panel in front of you. Any new press or stick push advances; a timeout also skips.
  3. When asked to press the left Touch menu (☰) button, press it. The session may pause or exit — that is expected and is the result we need.
  4. After VR ends, review the summary table, tap Download JSON, and send that file to Anika / Systems.

A copy is also stored in this browser as localStorage key sr_input_probe_v1. Hard-refresh this page before a retest so you are not on a cached copy.
